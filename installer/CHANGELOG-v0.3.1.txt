NEXORA v0.3.1 Tester

Self-introduction and Demo mode improvements:
- “give me self introduction” now receives the approved NEXORA introduction.
- Creator, feature, help, privacy, feedback, navigation, and greeting prompts have useful local replies.
- Unsupported questions explain that the online NEXORA service is required; no generic echo is shown.
- Tester builds can use a developer-operated HTTPS NEXORA service through deployment configuration while keeping provider controls hidden.
- Service tokens can be read from Windows Credential Manager and are never logged or bundled.
